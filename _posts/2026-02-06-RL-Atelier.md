RL-Atelier ([GitHub link here](https://github.com/charlypg/RL-Atelier/)) aims to remain as simple, open, and easy to extend as possible, making it well suited for crafting new ideas, learning by doing and teaching.

The aim of the library is to make reinforcement learning clear, approachable, and enjoyable to learn, by emphasizing modularity, transparency and strong functional principles thanks to [JAX](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/).

Contributions are welcome!
